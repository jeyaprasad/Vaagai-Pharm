Health guide

Understanding your numbers, daily habits, and practical steps to maintain healthy blood sugar.

Blood sugar
Measured 2 hours after a glucose drink. Under 140 mg/dL is typical. Fasting tests should typically stay below 100 mg/dL.
BMI & Weight
For Indian adults, a BMI of 23 or above indicates overweight, and 25 or above is higher risk. Even modest weight loss helps.
Blood pressure
Diastolic (the lower number) under 80 mmHg is typical. High blood pressure often occurs alongside high blood sugar.
Family history
Having parents or siblings with diabetes increases your risk due to shared genetics and household nutrition habits.

Early signs to watch for

Awareness
Unusual thirst

Feeling thirsty even after drinking plenty of water.

Frequent urination

Waking multiple times at night to pass urine.

Constant tiredness

Feeling exhausted even after getting adequate sleep.

Slow healing cuts

Small wounds, sores, or skin infections taking weeks to heal.

Small habits that make a big difference

Prevention
Brisk daily walk: Aim for 30 minutes of walking, 5 days each week.
Traditional millets: Replace white rice or maida with ragi, jowar, or bajra.
Reduce sweet drinks: Enjoy chai without sugar and drink water or fresh buttermilk.
Routine tests: Check fasting sugar and HbA1c at your Primary Health Centre.

When to get help

Emergency guidance, clinical advice, and what to do if you feel unwell.

Emergency (India)

Call emergency services: 108 or 112

In India, dial 108 for free ambulance or 112 for national helpline.

Seek immediate emergency attention if you or someone experiences:

  • Sudden weakness or numbness in face, arm, or leg (especially one side).
  • Severe chest pain, pressure, or difficulty breathing.
  • Extreme confusion, slurred speech, or loss of consciousness with severe sugar levels.
  • Persistent severe vomiting or inability to keep liquids down.

See a doctor soon (Non-emergency)

Clinical check

Visit a Primary Health Centre (PHC), Community Health Centre, or diabetologist if you notice:

  • Consistently elevated home sugar readings (fasting > 126 mg/dL or post-meal > 200 mg/dL).
  • Persistent tingling, numbness, or burning sensation in feet or hands.
  • Blurry vision that does not improve after resting.
  • Frequent recurring skin or urinary tract infections.
Please remember: Prediabetes and early high blood sugar are common and manageable. Taking action early through simple lifestyle changes and guidance from a local healthcare provider can protect your long-term health.

About Vaagai Pharm

Making diabetes screening approachable, transparent, and private for Indian families.

Why Vaagai Pharm exists

Our story

Vaagai (meaning victory tree / banyan in Tamil) symbolizes strength and longevity. Across India, millions of people live with early elevated blood sugar or prediabetes without knowing it. Vaagai Pharm was designed to provide an easy, question-by-question check that translates complex measurements into plain-language advice before health problems develop.

How it works in 3 steps

Process
1. Answer simple questions

Input your age, body measurements, blood sugar, and health history.

2. Pattern comparison

Your numbers are compared against established research patterns.

3. Plain-language advice

Receive clear explanations, top factors, and steps to discuss with your doctor.

What this cannot do (Important limitations)

Honesty

This tool is not a medical diagnosis and cannot prescribe medications or treatments.

The underlying study is based on 768 women of Pima heritage from Arizona, USA (the Pima are a Native American community, not people from India). Indian adults frequently develop diabetes at lower body weights and younger ages, so this estimate is only a rough guide. Always confirm your results with a doctor and a formal blood test at a Primary Health Centre or diagnostic laboratory.

Your privacy

Privacy

Your health numbers are checked on this computer. To write your simple advice, the numbers - never the reference label - are sent securely to an online writing service. Results are saved on this computer for the clinic summary. Please do not type your real name.
